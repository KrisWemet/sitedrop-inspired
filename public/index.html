Lead to landing page in three steps

The whole pipeline lives in one dashboard.

1

Discover no-website leads

Search any niche in any city — plumbers in Austin, salons in Portland. Every business missing a website is flagged hot, sorted to the top, with contact details ready for outreach.

2

Gather their info

One click compiles a full prospect profile: contact channels, opening hours, category intel, and ready-to-use positioning copy for their industry.

3

Generate their website

A complete, responsive, professionally designed site — hero, services, hours, contact — built from the gathered data. Preview it live, switch themes, download the file, close the deal.

Everything in the box

Zero dependencies. One command: npm start.

🔥 No-website auto-flag

Live OpenStreetMap data — absence of a website tag means a hot lead, scored and sorted.

📇 Contact extraction

Phone, email, address, and opening hours captured with every lead.

👀 Live preview
